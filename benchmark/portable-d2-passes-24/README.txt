Portable metadata for the original 24 D2 passes

Population:
  24 candidates from the original 52-project D2 set.

Meaning:
  Each candidate completed D2 using the recorded current-version
  Symmaries Maven plugin, API, worker, JSymCompiler/Soot frontend,
  sources-and-sinks policy, all.secstubs, and Syrs engine setup.

Contents:
  d2-passing-selection.csv
  d2-passing-sample.csv
  d2-passing-modules.csv
  d2-passing-artifacts.csv
  d2-passing-terminal-results.csv
  d2-passing-audit.csv
  candidate-ids.txt

Portability:
  This bundle contains metadata and results only.
  Absolute paths in artifact records may be device-specific.
  Candidate source repositories and binary JARs must be prepared
  independently on another evaluation device.
